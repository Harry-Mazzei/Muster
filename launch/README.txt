MUSTER - parade music console
=============================

FIRST TIME SETUP
1. Copy your music files into the "music" folder.
2. Start Muster:
   - Windows: double-click Muster.exe
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info > Run anyway.
   - Mac: right-click "Start Muster (Mac).command" > Open > Open.
     (Right-click is only needed the first time. After that, double-click.)
3. Muster opens in your web browser. Keep the black window open while you use it.
   Muster finds the beat points of each track the first time it sees it.

MOVING BETWEEN LAPTOPS
The whole Muster folder is portable. Copy it to a USB stick or another laptop and
your parades, beat points and settings come with it (they live in the "data" folder).
Settings > Download backup also saves everything (except music) to one file, which
you can restore on another laptop or in the web version.

ON PARADE
- GO (Space): play the next movement, or change to it on the next beat point.
- AS YOU WERE (Left arrow): cancel a pending stop/change, or undo the last start.
- STOP (Esc): stop on the next beat point. Press again to stop immediately.
- F fade out, S skip next, E emergency play, 1-9 favourites.
- Foot pedals that send Page Down / Page Up work as GO / STOP.
- Connect speakers by cable where you can. Bluetooth adds a delay; adjust
  "Beat point timing" in Settings if stops land early or late.
- If the laptop sleeps, Muster stops the music safely. Press GO to carry on
  from where it stopped.

QUITTING
Settings > Quit Muster, or close the black window.

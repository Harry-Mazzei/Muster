          Music & beat points

          The green handles mark where the music starts and ends; drag them to change it. Greyed-out parts are skipped everywhere in Muster (your files are never edited). Click the waveform to move the playhead. Beat points are the thick marks: STOP and GO changes land on them. Thin marks are detected beats. The music here plays through your speakers, so don't use it during a parade.
